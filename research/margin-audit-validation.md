# Opportunity Validation — Janitorial Margin Audit

## Hypothesis (revised after discovery)
**Problem:** Small commercial cleaning companies (10-50 employees) systematically underprice jobs → average 6.3% net margin (vs 15-20% achievable). The root cause: owners cannot explain what's in their price — no visibility into loaded labor rate, overhead recovery, or true job cost.

**Solution:** A 2-hour **Janitorial Margin Audit** — review their books/QuickBooks + run the numbers + deliver a 5-page report showing the exact line items leaking margin, presented with one corrective action per item.

**Price:** ₹9,999 (one-time, no subscription). Positions as "one bid audit that pays for itself."

**Why this works (evidence):**
1. **Market pain is proven & quantified:** SweptWorks "hidden costs" report + Stackrows pricing guide confirm — 6.3% industry avg net margin; the gap to 15-20% is underpricing + overhead blindness. Every cleaning site echoes this.
2. **No direct competitor on the audit angle:** BidForge ($197/mo), CleanGuru ($79-159/mo), QuoteIQ ($29.99-699/mo) are all *ongoing platforms*. Nobody sells a single diagnostic audit. This is a blue-ocean entry point into the stack.
3. **Payment friction = zero:** One-time fee, no monthly commitment → faster "yes" than a software subscription.
4. **Delivery ease = high:** Read their P&L / QB export → run ~12 line items through standard cleaning-cost model → 5-page report. Template reusable.
5. **Upsell path = clear:** After the audit, upsell either (a) CleanBid software setup to fix it, or (b) monthly repricing retainer (₹15k-25k/mo).
6. **Pricing credible:** ₹9,999 sits below agency rates ($75-300/hr) but well above "free consult" territory → taken seriously.

## Why this might fail
- Cleaning owners are price-sensitive and may see a ₹9,999 audit as "too expensive" if not positioned as "finds the leak that costs more."
- Decision-maker may not be the owner (could be bookkeeper). Need to reach the person who signs checks.
- Some companies may not have organized books to audit.

## How to position it (key message)
> "I find the 5-7 line items in your current pricing that are leaking 3-8% margin. If the audit doesn't show you exactly where to recover ₹50,000+ per year, you keep the fee."

## Target criteria (refined)
Commercial cleaning company, 10-50 employees, US/UK/EU (payment capable via UPI/ Razorpay/ PayPal), owner-operator or president reachable on LinkedIn, recent growth/hiring signals, uses QuickBooks/Excel for costs.
