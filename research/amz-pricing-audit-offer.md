# Opportunity #1 — Amazon/Flipkart Seller Pricing Audit Report

## The offer (smallest paid experiment)
**"30-Minute Pricing Audit Report" — ₹9,999**  
A 10-15 page PDF delivered in 48h:
1. Competitor price map of the top 5 ASINs vs client's SKU, across 30-60 day price history
2. Margin stress test — at current price vs lowest viable price (factoring Amazon/Flipkart fees + estimated COGS)
3. 3 concrete pricing recommendations with projected monthly revenue uplift (₹ impact)
4. "Dynamic repricing window" — when to auto-cut vs hold price based on Buy Box ownership

**Why it sells:**
- Amazon sellers *already* pay $100-500/listing for optimization; this is a scoped $120 slice.
- The deliverable is a concrete, auditable artifact (PDF) → no ambiguity about "done".
- Time-to-value = 48h → fits the cash-flow-constrained seller's urgency.
- Margin: my cost = 0 (I do the research), their risk = ₹0 (no setup), my risk = 2 days labour.
- Strong signal if they ask for "more SKUs" after the first report → natural upsell → ₹10k/mo retainer.
