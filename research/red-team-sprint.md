## 1. ATTACK OUR OWN OFFER — 5 Reasons an Owner Says No

| # | Objection | Surmountable? | Evidence base |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"My books are garbage — I just know my hourly labor."** Cleaning owners frequently fly blind on actual overhead (Swept hidden-costs report confirms 63% don't track overhead recovery). If books aren't clean, the audit can't find *precise* leaks → offer looks speculative. **NOT surmountable** without clean data → requires pre-audit bookkeeping step we don't price. |
| 2 | **"You're not my accountant — why should I trust you?"** No CPA/Certified Management Accountant credential → perceived as a "consultant" upsell, not a legitimate business expense. CFO/consultant alternatives carry recognized authority. **NOT surmountable** without professional designation or partnership with an accountant. |
| 3 | **₹9,999 > my last bid profit.** If a typical contract = ₹80,000-1,50,000 annual value and net margin = ₹8,000-15,000/year, a ₹9,999 audit fee is 60-100% of their annual profit. Feels like "pay 6 months of profit to save 6 months of profit" → no deal. **Requires repositioning as "one contract audit" not "business audit."** |
| 4 | **"BidForge/CleanGuru/QuoteIQ already shows my margins live"** — These tools embed real-time margin views. An owner who already uses BidForge (₹197/mo, "margin guardrails", "why this price" breakdown) has no gap to fill → we compete with a tool they're already paying for. **NOT surmountable** for existing software users. |
| 5 | **"I can just Google 'janitorial loaded labor rate' + use a template"** The core calculation (wage × 1.25 burden × 40 hrs × visits) is 3rd-grade math. An owner with 10 years experience trusts their gut + Excel more than a stranger's 5-page PDF. **NOT surmountable** without social proof + guaranteed-find framing. |

**VERDICT:** 3/5 objections are NOT surmountable. Offer needs either (a) a credential anchor (CPA partner), or (b) a guaranteed-find/refund guarantee, or (c) repackaging as a contract-specific bid audit.

## 4. SAMPLE AUDIT (using SweptWorks.com published cleaning pricing report — fully public)

### "Hidden Costs That Kill Janitorial Profits" — Public Report Analysis
*Source: learn.sweptworks.com/hidden-costs-cleaning-pricing (public content)*

#### Audit finding: "Ghost Hours" — Scheduling Inefficiency
**PUBLIC FACT:** SweptWorks reports cleaners bill 7.2 hours/day but schedule 8.0 hours/day (87.5% utilization — 12.5% unbilled).
**CALCULATION (assumption):** If cleaner wage = ₹300/hr burdened, 12.5% × 1 cleaner × ₹300 × 20 days = ₹7,500/month unbilled.
**HYPOTHESIS:** A company with 5 cleaners loses ₹37,500/month to unbilled travel/wait time. Annual = ₹4,50,000.
**CUSTOMER PROMISE:** "We'd find 3-5 sites per cleaners where travel/setup eats 10-15 min daily. Routing 5 cleaners onto shared 2 sites saves ₹4.5L/year."

#### Audit finding: Labor cost creep without adjustment
**PUBLIC FACT:** Stackrows: wage $15/hr base → $18/hr loaded; 3% annual wage inflation typical.
**CALCULATION:** 3% wage creep × 40% labor margin = 1.2% annual margin erosion. On ₹10L contract = ₹12,000/year lost.
**HYPOTHESIS:** Fixed-price contracts signed 2 years ago now run at 6% margin vs original 12% target.
**CUSTOMER PROMISE:** "Your 2024 renewal bids underpriced by 8% due to wage creep you didn't adjust for."

#### Audit finding: Overhead recovery
**PUBLIC FACT:** Stackrows: overhead = 15-25% of revenue; many owners exclude it from bids.
**CALCULATION:** ₹2000/month software + ₹5000/month insurance + ₹8000/month vehicle = ₹15,000. On ₹50,000/month revenue = 30% overhead — NOT recovered.
**HYPOTHESIS:** Business runs at break-even on paper but owner draws ₹0 because overhead bleeds profit.
**CUSTOMER PROMISE:** "You're billing at 6% net because your quotes exclude ₹15k of monthly overhead. Fix the burden rate and add ₹3k/month to every proposal."

## 6. 3 ALTERNATIVE OFFERS

### A. Low-risk paid diagnostic
- **Price:** ₹4,999
- **Deliverable:** 3-page "5 Red Flags in Your Quotes" report (top 5 margin leaks from their numbers)
- **Time:** 1 hour review + 1 hr report
- **Promise:** "Pay ₹4,999. If we don't identify 3 concrete margin improvements worth ₹15,000+, you keep the fee."
- **Big objection:** "Feels like a lead-gen bait — what's the real upsell?"
- **Why better:** Lower price = higher trial. 4× more conversions expected. Still ≥₹10k = 2 sales.

### B. Performance/implementation offer
- **Price:** ₹19,999 (or "3 months risk free")
- **Deliverable:** Full margin remediation — we re-price 3 of your active contracts and document the savings, then implement new burden rates in your system (CleanGuru/Google Sheets)
- **Time:** 4 hours analysis + 2 hrs implementation
- **Promise:** "We reprice 3 contracts. If total annual savings < ₹1,00,000, you pay nothing."
- **Big objection:** "You want access to my systems + contracts = security concern."
- **Why better:** Outcome-based = no-brainer if true. But harder to close initially (trust + access barriers).

### C. Software-integrated micro-offer
- **Price:** ₹0 (free) + ₹1,499/mo monitoring
- **Deliverable:** Weekly margin dashboard (Google Sheet + email) tracking their actual vs. target margins per contract
- **Time:** Set up once, monitor passively
- **Promise:** "We track your numbers weekly and alert you when a contract drops below 8% margin."
- **Big objection:** "Another monthly subscription I'll cancel in 6 months."
- **Why better:** Recurring = predictable revenue. But ₹0 upfront = no immediate ₹10k.

## 7. FINAL VERDICT

### OFFER STATUS: 🟡 MODIFY

### Strongest evidence *for*:
- Market pain is real + quantified (6.3% → 15-20% net margin gap)
- Competitors exist but none sells a **one-off diagnostic audit** specifically → positioning gap
- SweptWorks, Stackrows, Janitorial.com *all* publish exactly the analysis we'd deliver → content legitimacy

### Strongest evidence *against*:
- **3/5 major objections are NOT surmountable** without (a) professional credential, (b) guaranteed-find framing, or (c) integration with existing software stack
- Direct competitors (BidForge, CleanGuru) already do live in-app margin diagnostics for ₹197-5,000/yr
- The segment that most needs this (₹6L contracts, ₹38k profit) perceives ₹9,999 as unaffordable
- No recognized business-credential backing the diagnosis → trust barrier

### Biggest unsupported assumption:
That a cleaning company owner will pay a **stranger without a CPA/Certified Management Accountant credential** ₹9,999 for financial analysis — when QuickBooks + a $150/hr CPA already offers the same service with recognized authority.

### Ideal customer:
- **Franchise owner** (Jan-Pro, Merry Maids) OR **regional multi-location chain** (5-15 locations)
- Annual revenue: ₹30L-1.5Cr
- Uses QuickBooks but *not* integrated bidding software (no BidForge/CleanGuru)
- Recently lost a bid or had a contract renewal dispute
- Active on LinkedIn → reachable

### Recommended price:
**₹4,999** for a 3-page "margin leak report" with a **money-back guarantee** ("if we don't find 3 margin improvements worth ₹15k, the fee is refunded").

### Recommended first sales channel:
**LinkedIn cold DM to franchise owners** (Jan-Pro, Merry Maids, etc.) — they are the most margin-sensitive and the most reachable.
